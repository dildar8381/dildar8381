Principles

Assistants should answer from approved sources. Retrieval, citations and clear fallbacks keep responses trustworthy.

Design for hand-off

No bot knows everything. A smooth transfer to a person, with context attached, protects the user's time and trust.

FAQ

Questions people often ask.

What does a chatbot project usually involve?

It starts with understanding who the users are and what they ask for most. From there I design intents, entities and conversation flows in Dialogflow ES or CX, connect the bot to your systems through APIs, train and test it with real phrases, and deploy it. After launch I review real conversations and keep improving accuracy.

How do you keep generative AI answers accurate?

I ground the model in your own content with retrieval-augmented generation, so answers come from approved sources instead of guesses. I add fallbacks for low-confidence questions, hand-off to a person when needed, and review real conversations to improve the content and prompts over time.

Which systems can you integrate with?

Salesforce, Workday, Banner, student information systems (SIS), learning management systems (LMS) and IT service management (ITSM) tools, using REST APIs built on Node.js.

Can you build voicebots as well as chat?

Yes. I work with Twilio for voice and write prompts and confirmations that sound natural when spoken, with routing to a human agent or a back-end system when needed.

How do you keep applications fast?

I cache frequent lookups in Redis, use Elasticsearch for quick search, keep APIs lean with Node.js and MongoDB, and split code on the React.js front end so each page loads only what it needs.

How do you ship and run what you build?

Services are containerized with Docker, deployed on AWS behind Nginx, and released through CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions, so updates are repeatable and low risk.
